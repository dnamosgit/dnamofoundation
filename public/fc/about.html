About Us

The Dnamo Way

Excellence in academics and soccer builds maturity and self-confidence.

Dnamo FC believes the experiences a player has with us will be beneficial throughout their lifetime. We are resolute in dedicating our time and resources to the technical, tactical, physical and psychological aspects of playing soccer.

Our mission

Our mission is simple: we strive to inspire our youth, on and off the field, to be better people and players. Focusing on player development and the love of the game, we empower our members to be positive and skilled athletes.

Dnamo FC serves the more committed soccer player by providing the highest level of education and instruction available, preparing players to proudly represent the club at local, state, regional and national competitions.

Development

Developing soccer players is a long-term process that requires passion and commitment from professional coaches and a community of members who genuinely care. We are invested in your children from the start, and seeing them flourish and reach their full potential is our reward.

Our community

We look to harness the ability of our members and give them opportunities to excel on and off the field. We want a reputation for developing exceptional human beings while helping those who wish to move on to programs of the highest caliber.

Meet our team

Our coaching staff is nationally licensed and continually attending professional development programs. Our coaches are supportive of each child, knowledgeable in player development, and hired for their approach, mindset and experience. Select a coach to read their profile.

Driven by a desire to give back to the sport that changed his life, Ika Kvavilashvili founded Dnamo FC. His vision was to create a platform where young athletes could experience the same opportunities and guidance that shaped his own journey. From his roots at Dinamo Academy in Georgia to winning titles like the Dallas Cup and Surf Cup with Santa Clara Sporting in the US, his journey is a testament to his talent and tenacity. Ika draws on his experiences from Europe and the US to positively impact young athletes. While testing his skills professionally in Turkey and Georgia, coaching emerged as his true calling.

Claudio Castro, a revered Bay Area coach, began his journey with Mt. Diablo Soccer Club and later excelled with Diablo Valley Soccer Club as an ODP Regional Player. He reached the Futsal Nationals semi-final in 2007 and led Concord High's NCS Championship run in 2012-2013 as top goal scorer. In 2023, all four teams he coached made State Cup finals, with three winning, marking his coaching at NorCal's highest levels.

Coach Paulo has more than 12 years of coaching experience. His soccer journey began as a goalkeeper in Peru's top academies, earning a spot on the U-15 national team. At Dnamo FC he channels that experience into shaping goalkeepers and teams, instilling discipline and teamwork and fostering a culture of excellence.

Benno Nagel brings more than 15 years of experience across youth development and the professional game, including work with the Dinamo Zagreb Academy in Croatia and Rayo OKC of the former North American Soccer League. He has also contributed to the launch of professional clubs such as Oakland Roots SC and The Town FC, giving him a broad view of the pathway from youth soccer to the highest levels.

Guillaume, also known as "Ganso," comes from Suresnes in West Paris. His soccer journey began at five, inspired by PSG and Zinedine Zidane. He played for local clubs and started coaching with Dnamo FC in 2021, working from Microsoccer up to NorCal competitive levels. Coaching lets him stay connected to the game while passing on life lessons and nurturing young players on and off the field.

Coach Luis, originally from Guatemala, arrived in the United States at the age of two. His soccer journey began at six with America Scores. He won the State Cup in 2014-2015 and the CIF Championship with Lowell High School. Luis coaches to give youngsters the same opportunities he cherished, guiding both recreational and travel teams at Dnamo.

Ahmed Fara began coaching in high school and joined Dnamo Football Club in 2022. His work is marked by a commitment to excellence and holistic player development, instilling discipline, teamwork and perseverance so that young athletes can meet life's challenges with resilience.

Alberto Toledo, from San Francisco, excelled as a goalkeeper and captained his high school team. He played at the highest level in NorCal from age 14 and competed in Europe's Donosti Cup. A Molecular Biology graduate of UC Santa Cruz, he is now a goalkeeper coach.

Born and raised in San Francisco, Alex's passion for football grew while playing at club level. He began coaching at Dnamo in 2022. Alex is deeply interested in the tactical side of the game and draws on the ideas of managers like Mikel Arteta, Pep Guardiola and Jose Mourinho in his coaching.

Coach Armando (Mando) started playing fútbol in Colombia at the age of three and played for his city team from U10 to U15 in semi-professional tournaments. He moved to the U.S. at 16 and played two years of varsity soccer. As a coach he has learned that playing is not the only reason to love fútbol.

Diego Sanchez played for the Guatemalan national team and Mission Green FC, with appearances at the Donosti Cup, Dallas Cup and Surf Cup. He holds a D coaching license and is working toward his C license.

Originally from São Paulo, Coach Douglas brings an 11-year professional playing career, notably with Atlético Goianiense, and a sophisticated tactical approach. Blending the creative flair of Brazilian football with modern coaching standards, he develops well-rounded athletes who are technically precise and tactically aware, both outfield players and goalkeepers.

For Coach Gaby, football is the sport of his life. He joined his local club in France at six, discovered his qualities as a goalkeeper, and at ten joined the Tours Football Club academy, where he spent eight years from U10 to U18. He is now a youth coach in San Francisco who takes joy in sharing his knowledge and passion.

Coach Jesús started playing in Colombia at four, spent five years in Spain, and made his professional debut in Colombia at 17. He went on to play professionally in Chile, Mexico and the United States. He began coaching in the U.S. in 2016 and loves passing his knowledge on to children, helping them grow in football and as people.

Juan Larios started playing at three and went on to four years of collegiate soccer at Cal State San Bernardino, reaching the NCAA Division II Regional Championship. He has coached at Dnamo FC for three years and aims to make a meaningful impact in the lives of his players.

Coach Michael was born and raised in San Francisco and joined Dnamo FC as a player at 13. Through the club he played at the highest level of youth soccer in the U.S., including MLS Next with the SF Glens. He now plays at City College of San Francisco and coaches at the club that helped him succeed.

Paolo, originally from Paris, trained at professional youth academies alongside future professionals. For him coaching is a true vocation: mentorship that develops skill, nurtures a real love for the game and teaches life lessons. He joined Dnamo after a year in Berkeley.

Coach Roberto grew up in Brazil, left home at 11 to join an academy and signed his first professional contract at 15 with Atlético Mineiro. As a goalkeeper he played in the Campeonato Brasileiro Série A and Série B with clubs like Goiás and Atlético Goianiense, and gained international experience with Udinese in Italy. He now helps young athletes build strong skills and a deep understanding of the game.

Coach Rodrigo, born and raised in Bahia, Brazil, developed his skills in the youth academy of Vitória FC. He began coaching in 2014 with Soccer Without Borders and has coached in San Francisco since 2016 with middle school, high school and club programs, building confidence and love for the game.

Coach Salvador grew up playing in Mexico City from the age of six. He began coaching ten years ago in San Leandro and Hayward. A father of three who all play soccer, he values discipline, teamwork and personal growth, and trains players of different ages to reach their full potential.
