Competitive

For players ready to train more seriously and compete at a higher level.

OverviewJuniorsMicrosoccerRecreationalCompetitiveTravelGoalkeeper AcademyIDPReferee
Ages
U9 - U18
Entry
Tryouts or evaluation
Coaches
Licensed staff

Players are placed on teams through evaluations or tryouts and train in structured environments led by licensed coaches. The program emphasizes technical development, tactical understanding and competitive performance, with league play and tournaments through the season.

We place players where they will be challenged and where they will play. Both matter.

TryoutsFees

Focus areas

  • Technical mastery
  • Tactical understanding
  • Competitive mindset
  • Team development

Typical structure

  • U9-U12: 1 training and 1 conditioning session per week
  • U13 and up: 2 trainings and 1 conditioning session per week
  • A week of pre-season training
  • League play and tournaments (NPL, Premier, Gold, Silver, Bronze and Upper House levels)

What comes next

Our most committed players move into the Travel program, and can add specialist work through the Goalkeeper Academy or the Individual Development Program.
