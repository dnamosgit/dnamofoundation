Team training builds chemistry, tactical understanding and competitive identity. It remains the foundation of our development model. But meaningful individual growth often needs additional structure: accountability, detailed film analysis and long-term planning.

The IDP is an optional high-performance pathway for ambitious field players. It is not additional team training. It is individualized strategic development. Goalkeepers have their own IDP within the Goalkeeper Academy.

Register

Who it is for

  • Competitive youth players seeking additional structure
  • Motivated young players who want a long-term development plan
  • Players targeting varsity impact roles
  • College-aspiring athletes
  • Players pursuing professional pathways

What players do

Each player is paired with a performance coach who serves as strategist, mentor and accountability partner. Through in-person and virtual sessions, players will:

  • Build a personalized annual development roadmap
  • Set measurable performance objectives
  • Conduct structured film analysis and tactical reviews
  • Develop professional habits and accountability systems
  • Receive monthly written progress evaluations
  • Join exclusive webinars with professional players and coaches

Program structure

Month 1 · Assessment and foundationDevelopment intake and goal assessment, parent-player-coach strategy meeting, on-field technical evaluation, tactical film breakdown and an initial development report
Every month · Guided developmentFour weekly 45-minute virtual performance sessions, one 60-minute on-field session, a written progress report and one live professional webinar

Program leadership

The program is led by Benno Nagel, a former professional coach with more than 15 years in advanced youth development and professional environments, including Dinamo Zagreb Academy (Croatia) and Rayo OKC of the North American Soccer League. He has worked with nearly 40 national team players across youth and senior levels, including several who have competed in FIFA World Cup competitions.

During this pilot phase Benno works directly with every participating player, and connects with each player's team coaches so the individual work reinforces their team environment.

Investment and value

In the Bay Area, private training typically costs $100 to $150 per hour, without film analysis, written evaluations or structured mentorship. The IDP combines all of these in one framework. Spots are intentionally limited.
